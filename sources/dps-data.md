## Notes for the build
- The sheet has additional columns not reproduced here (base damage, crit rate per skill, with-addons/sov multipliers, summon-damage variants). They're in `dps-sheet-raw.csv` if the app needs them.
- "Cancelled" / "fast" rows are the same skill measured with an animation cancel — that's why a
  skill like Foxspirit Tag or Petal Play appears multiple times at different DPS. The cancelled
  version is the one used in optimised play.
- Foxflare Fling (the Magnus skill) is listed at every hit-count; the Discord/video both say to leave
  Magnus unlearned for PvE, so these rows mainly exist for completeness.
- To refresh: re-run the CSV export
  `curl -sL "https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1F6D6zCWd7w0hgTnKrtvrPxVEqMj5qT2-sXdbXvXc1UQ/export?format=csv&gid=513999560"`
